Help from the touchline

For help with SevenSide, email alisahinakbaba@icloud.com. Include your app version, iPhone or iPad model and a description of the issue. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.

  • Use landscape orientation. Players move automatically; the match buttons send attacking or defensive commands.
  • Continue career loads your saved club. Progress is stored on your device, without a SevenSide account.
  • Starting a new career replaces the previous career. Keep your existing career when resolving a pending purchase.
  • The music button controls background music. Match sound effects are separate.

Optional in-app purchases

Currency packs and special players are consumables tied to the purchasing career. Game euros are virtual currency with no cash value. A special player can be purchased once per career and ages and retires through normal gameplay. Purchases do not transfer to a new career.

Apple displays the local price before payment. The game never asks for payment card details. Purchases require an internet connection to Apple's services.

If payment was interrupted, keep the original career and open Career → Store → Check purchases. This recovers unfinished transactions; it does not restore completed consumables or move them to a new career. Deleting the app or losing its saved data may permanently remove progress and delivered purchases.

For a missing purchase, contact support with the Apple order reference if available. For refund requests, visit Apple's Report a Problem. Apple decides refund eligibility.

Privacy policy

SevenSide: Soccer Manager — Privacy and Support. Effective 8 November 2026. Developer: Ali Şahin Akbaba (Pentafly Games). Contact: alisahinakbaba@icloud.com.

SevenSide is a single-player game. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. We do not send your career, club name, manager name, game results or settings to a developer server.

Career progress, settings and purchase-delivery records are stored on your device. The iOS app also keeps a private atomic career save to recover interrupted writes. Starting a new career replaces the previous career. Deleting the app or losing its saved data may permanently remove progress and delivered purchases.

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles billing under its privacy policy. We do not receive payment card details. The app verifies Apple transactions and stores their identifiers and a randomly generated career identifier on-device to deliver each purchase once to the purchasing career.

Currency packs and special players are consumables for the purchasing career only. Game euros have no cash value. Each special player can be purchased once per career, ages and retires. Purchases do not transfer to a new career. Completed consumables cannot be restored from Apple; Check purchases recovers unfinished transactions to their original saved career.

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to answer your request. Do not send your Apple password, card details or other credentials. Include the app version, device model and a description of the problem. For missing purchases, include the Apple order reference if available. Refund decisions are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Support email: alisahinakbaba@icloud.com. For purchase approval requests, keep the original career until the request is resolved. To stop music use the menu music button; to resume a saved career use Continue career. Device-level purchase restrictions are managed in Apple Screen Time.

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change. Apple privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.
